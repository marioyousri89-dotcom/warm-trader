# Put it online in about 10 minutes (no building on your computer)
1. Make a free GitHub account, create a new PRIVATE repository, and upload everything in this folder (drag and drop on github.com).
2. Make a Render.com account > New > Blueprint > choose that repository > Apply.
3. In Render, open the service > Environment: paste your ANTHROPIC_API_KEY. Copy the generated DASHBOARD_TOKEN (this is your password).
4. Open https://<your-service>.onrender.com/?token=<DASHBOARD_TOKEN> on your phone and "Add to Home Screen". That is the app.
Cost: roughly US$7/month for the always-on plan plus Claude API usage. It already runs with `--live`: real Binance prices, real Polymarket odds and Yahoo stock data, and the agents do their
education round on real recent history first. All trading is still PAPER money.
